# Limitations checklist

Each item says what the limitation is, the evidence in this repo, and how it should be worded in the report.

- [ ] **Selective labels.** In deployment only reviewed alerts receive labels; here every positive is labelled, so the calibration miss rate is measurable. A deployed certificate needs shadow-mode sampling outside the top K. Evidence: design of `src/certify.py` (all positives available). Wording: "the certificate assumes labels for all positives, which a live system only gets via sampling".
- [ ] **IBM volume artifact and the Sep 10 cutoff.** Normal traffic collapses after 2022-09-10 while laundering continues, so only transactions before 2022-09-11 are used; 655 of 5,177 laundering rows (and 1,108 transactions) are dropped. Evidence: `report/numbers.md` section 9; `src/config.py` (`ACTIVE_END`). The cutoff was chosen after seeing the volume series, before modelling.
- [ ] **UNATTRIBUTED positives: 38% of laundering transactions and 45.7% of positive entity-periods.** They belong to no listed pattern attempt, so typology is unknown; the pooled certificate under-serves them (pooled exchangeable coverage 0.00). Evidence: `report/numbers.md` section 9 and 3a. Wording: UNATTRIBUTED is "no listed pattern", not a single typology.
- [ ] **Dependence between consecutive periods for the same account.** Entity-periods of one account are not independent, which weakens the exchangeability the bound needs; the exchangeable controls here re-split entity-period miss flags at random and do not model this. Evidence: `src/split.py` output (accounts in more than one split).
- [ ] **Temporal split overlap: 71% of accounts appear in more than one split.** Component-aware splitting was infeasible (giant component 72% yet spanning all periods). Evidence: `README.md` Data and modelling choices.
- [ ] **Multiplicity: 36 (typology, budget) cells per scorer and delta.** Even with valid bounds about 10% of cells would miss by chance at delta = 0.10 (about 3-4 of 36), so individual cell failures are not evidence of structure; read patterns across budgets. Evidence: `results/coverage_by_typology*.csv`.
- [ ] **ACH dominance in the transaction model.** Payment format is the top feature (about 30% of gain); the IBM generator concentrates laundering in ACH, so the transaction model's strength is partly a generator artifact. Evidence: `src/transaction_model.py` output; `results/h3_summary.md`. Wording: the max-over-transaction advantage is demonstrated on synthetic data.
- [ ] **Elliptic tiny post-shutdown steps.** After step 43 several steps have very few illicit nodes (2 to 56; steps 45 and 46 have 5 and 2), so per-step error indicators are noisy and recovery lags are coarse. Evidence: `results/elliptic_spike.csv`.
- [ ] **One drift event, one dataset.** H4 rests on a single shutdown in Elliptic; the IBM delay check has only four evaluation periods and is supplementary. Evidence: `results/ibm_delay_check.md`.
- [ ] **Gate-specification correction after the first run.** The first gate compared a bound with another sample's observed rate, which cannot reach nominal coverage even for a valid bound (ceiling about 0.82 at delta 0.10); the gate was corrected to compare against the true or whole-population miss rate and was not changed afterwards. Evidence: `README.md` (Validity gate specification), `src/validity_gate.py` docstring, `results/validity_gate.csv` (`gating` column).
- [ ] **Post-hoc additions after the first freeze.** The finer H2 drift grid, the ACI-offset arm and the primary-scorer H1 rerun were added after seeing results (tag `results-frozen-v2`); they are labelled post hoc in the CSVs and the README. The headline-scorer choice itself was made on calibration data only.
- [ ] **Fixed-count budgets reviewed unequal shares of entities** in busy and quiet periods; the fraction budget did not remove the under-coverage, so volume is not the explanation, but fixed counts remain a limitation for real operations. Evidence: `results/volume_decomposition.md`.
- [ ] **Mixed currencies and synthetic data.** Amounts are standardised within currency using train statistics; nothing here says how the method behaves on a real bank's data.
