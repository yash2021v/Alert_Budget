# Report figure captions

Validity condition for every certified bound below: it covers the true miss rate with probability at least 1 - delta only if positives seen at deployment are exchangeable with the calibration positives. Temporal drift and dependence between periods can break this.

**Fig 1 - Validity gate** (`Fig1_validity_gate.png`, from `src/validity_gate.py`, data `results/validity_gate.csv`). Realised coverage of the true (or whole-population) miss rate by the Clopper-Pearson bound over 1,000 trials per configuration, against nominal coverage; the grey band spans nominal minus two standard errors to nominal. Green dots are the 18 gating configurations (synthetic data with a known truth, and a random 10% subsample of real calibration positives against all calibration positives); grey dots are informational. All gating configurations pass, which shows the code is correct under forced exchangeability; it says nothing about deployment.

**Fig 2 - Fixed-count vs fraction-of-volume budget** (`Fig2_budget_fixed_vs_fraction.png`, from `src/make_report_figures.py`, data `results/miss_rate_certified_vs_realised.csv` and `results/miss_rate_fraction_budget.csv`, direct scorer, delta 0.10). Certified bound (blue) and realised test miss rate (orange) against the budget as a share of the average period, calibrated on periods 12-15 and evaluated on periods 16-19. The bound is below the realised miss at every budget under both budget definitions, and the gap is larger under fractions, so period volume does not explain the under-coverage.

**Fig 3 - Group-conditional certificates by typology** (`Fig3_h1_typology_primary.png`, from `src/run_h1.py --primary`, data `results/coverage_by_typology_primary.csv`, primary scorer, delta 0.10, four budgets). Per-typology Clopper-Pearson bound (blue) against the realised test miss rate (orange) with the pooled bound as a dashed line; UNATTRIBUTED positives sit well above the pooled bound. Each group bound needs exchangeability within its group, and the pooled bound additionally needs all groups to share one miss rate.

**Fig 4 - H2 synthetic delay and drift** (`Fig4_h2_synthetic_delay.png`, from `src/h2_synthetic.py`, data `results/h2_synthetic_delay.csv`). Coverage of the true miss rate by a certificate built from the four most recent labelled periods, against label delay (left) and drift speed (right), with two-standard-error bars and the nominal 0.90 line; hollow/dashed series are the post-hoc finer drift grid. Without drift delay costs nothing; with slow drift coverage falls as the delay grows, and at faster drift coverage is already at the floor at zero delay.

**Fig 5 - H4 centerpiece on Elliptic** (`Fig5_h4_centerpiece.png`, from `src/h4_elliptic.py`, data `results/h4_elliptic_arms.csv`, delay L = 2, budget 10% of labelled nodes per step). Realised miss rate and bound per step for the static, rolling, ACI and (post-hoc) ACI-offset certificates; red rings mark steps where the binomial error indicator fires, the dashed line marks the step-43 shutdown, and the strip below shows ACI's delta_t and the offset. No arm recovers coverage after the shutdown within the 15 evaluation steps; the bound is valid only for steps exchangeable with its calibration pool.
