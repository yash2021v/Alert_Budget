# Claims audit

Status: **SUPPORTED** (the data back the claim as worded), **PARTLY SUPPORTED** (true with the stated caveat), **NOT SUPPORTED** (the data do not back it, or contradict it). Numbers are in `report/numbers.md`; results files are under `results/`.

| # | Claim (README / report / resume) | Status | Evidence and caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "The certificate is valid under exchangeability" | SUPPORTED | `validity_gate.csv`: all 18 gating configurations at or above nominal minus 2 SE (synthetic coverage 0.914-0.968); exchangeable controls in `h3_aggregation.csv`, `coverage_by_typology*.csv`, `elliptic_spike_control.csv`. Caveat: the gate specification was corrected after its first run (documented in README). |
| 2 | "The certificate breaks under temporal shift on IBM" | SUPPORTED | `miss_rate_certified_vs_realised.csv`: bound covers the realised test miss at 0/7 budgets for both scorers; `shift_diagnostic.md`: coverage returns when time order is removed. |
| 3 | "Period-volume variation explains the IBM under-coverage" | NOT SUPPORTED | `volume_decomposition.md`: the fraction-of-volume budget covers 0/7 budgets and the gap is larger (+0.066 vs +0.040 on average). |
| 4 | "Coverage breaks at the Elliptic shutdown (step 43)" | SUPPORTED | `elliptic_spike.csv/.md`, `h4_elliptic_summary.csv`: static error frequency 0.00 before and 0.71 from step 43 (L = 2, q = 10%); exchangeable control stays valid (0.97). Caveats: one dataset and one event; post-shutdown steps have few illicit nodes (2-56). |
| 5 | "UNATTRIBUTED is under-served by the pooled bound" | SUPPORTED | `coverage_by_typology_primary.csv` and `coverage_by_typology.csv`: pooled exchangeable coverage 0.00 at every budget for both scorers; `h1_summary.md`. Caveat: UNATTRIBUTED means "in no listed pattern attempt", not one typology. |
| 6 | "BIPARTITE and SCATTER-GATHER are under-served by the pooled bound" | PARTLY SUPPORTED | `h1_summary.md`: SCATTER-GATHER is never under-served in the exchangeable protocol (primary and direct); BIPARTITE is under-served at 1 of 4 budgets (primary, exchangeable coverage as low as 0.29) and 2 of 4 (direct). The temporal-split misses are mostly drift, not structure. |
| 7 | "Group-conditional bounds are valid" | PARTLY SUPPORTED | Exchangeable coverage 0.957-1.000 (`coverage_by_typology_primary.csv`, `exch_coverage`) but on the temporal split group bounds cover only 11 of 36 (typology, budget) cells at delta 0.10 (primary). |
| 8 | "The guarantee survives aggregation (max over transactions)" | SUPPORTED | `h3_aggregation.csv`, `h3_summary.md`: exchangeable control coverage 0.964-0.995 for the max score. Caveat: the temporal split still covers 0/7 budgets, as for the direct score. |
| 9 | "Max-over-transaction scoring finds more positives than the entity-level score" | PARTLY SUPPORTED | `h3_aggregation.csv`: recall higher at all 7 budgets (+17 points on average); chosen on calibration data only (`headline_scorer.md`). Caveats: the transaction model leans on payment format (ACH), a generator artifact; synthetic data; the learned-aggregator comparison was cut. |
| 10 | "Stale labels cost coverage" | PARTLY SUPPORTED | `h2_synthetic_delay.csv`, `h2_summary.md`: at slow drift (d = 0.005-0.02, a post-hoc grid) coverage falls with L; at d >= 0.05 coverage is on the floor even at L = 0; control d = 0 is unaffected by L. Synthetic only; the real IBM delay check (4 periods, supplementary) shows no delay effect. |
| 11 | "Rolling recalibration restores coverage after the shutdown" | NOT SUPPORTED | `h4_elliptic_summary.csv` (L = 2, q = 10%): error frequency from step 43 is 0.86 for rolling vs 0.71 for static; recovery lag none. |
| 12 | "ACI restores coverage" | NOT SUPPORTED | `h4_elliptic_summary.csv`, `h4_summary.md`: ACI (gamma 0.05) error frequency from step 43 is 0.86 at L = 2, q = 10%; recovery lag none at all L and q; gamma 0.01 and 0.10 identical. Adapting delta has little authority (`bound_vs_delta.csv`). |
| 13 | "An additive-offset ACI variant restores coverage" (post hoc) | NOT SUPPORTED | `h4_summary.md`: under the pre-set rule (error frequency after step 43 <= 0.20 with a finite recovery lag) it succeeds in 0 of 6 (L, q) settings; error frequency 0.86 at L = 2, q = 10%. |
| 14 | "The offset variant reduces errors at zero delay at the cost of looser bounds" | PARTLY SUPPORTED | `h4_elliptic_arms.csv`/`h4_elliptic_summary.csv`: at L = 0, q = 20% error frequency after 43 is 0.29 (lag 3) with max bound 0.97; at L = 0, q = 10% bound reaches 1.00 yet error frequency stays 0.57. Post hoc, one dataset. |
| 15 | "Features and scorers are leakage-free" | SUPPORTED | `tests/test_features.py`, `tests/test_txn_leakage.py`: features unchanged when later transactions are deleted or the label is shuffled or removed. |
| 16 | "The scorers detect laundering well" | PARTLY SUPPORTED | Entity-level test ROC-AUC 0.865 but PR-AUC 0.046 (`day1_model_metrics.json`); transaction model ROC-AUC 0.975 and PR-AUC 0.316. Synthetic data, and recall at small budgets is low. |
| 17 | "The binomial error indicator has error rate at most 0.10 under a valid bound" | SUPPORTED | `tests/test_coverage_monitor.py`. |
