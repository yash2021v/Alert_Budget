# Volume decomposition

Under the fixed-count budget (the primary definition) 0 of 7 budgets are covered at delta 0.10 and 0 of 7 at delta 0.05; under the fraction-of-volume budget 0 of 7 and 0 of 7.

The realised-minus-bound gap at delta 0.10 averages +0.040 (range +0.003 to +0.100) for fixed counts and +0.066 (range +0.005 to +0.137) for fractions.

Making the budget a share of each period's volume does not change how many budgets are covered, so period-volume variation alone does not explain the under-coverage.

The gap does not shrink under fractions, so volume is not the main driver and the remaining gap reflects score or positive-mix drift between calibration and test.

Fixed-count stays the primary budget definition; the fraction arm is a diagnostic and was not used to change it.
